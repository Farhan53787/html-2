Mathematics

is the study of Numbers, Shapes, and Patterns. It is a fundamental discipline that provides a framework for understanding the world around us. 
It includes operations like Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, Exponentiation, Rooting, Logarithm, and Integrations.
Mathematics is used in various fields, including science, engineering, economics, and technology. It helps us solve problems, make predictions, and analyze data. From basic arithmetic to advanced calculus, mathematics is a powerful tool that enables us to explore and comprehend the complexities of the universe.
